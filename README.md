# Vangti-chai ☕

A delightful chai (tea) experience application that brings the warmth and comfort of traditional tea culture to your digital world.

## 🌟 Overview

Vangti-chai is a modern application designed to celebrate and explore the rich world of chai and tea culture. Whether you're a tea enthusiast, a casual chai drinker, or someone looking to discover new flavors, Vangti-chai provides the perfect platform to enhance your tea journey.

## ✨ Features

- **Tea Discovery**: Explore a diverse collection of chai and tea varieties from around the world
- **Brewing Guides**: Learn the perfect brewing techniques for different types of tea
- **Flavor Profiles**: Discover detailed flavor notes and characteristics
- **Recipe Collection**: Access traditional and modern chai recipes
- **Brewing Timer**: Get perfectly timed brewing sessions for optimal flavor
- **Personal Tea Journal**: Track your favorite blends and brewing preferences
